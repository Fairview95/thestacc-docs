theStacc is an AI-powered marketing automation platform that helps small businesses get found online. It handles SEO content creation, local search optimization, and social media management — all on autopilot.

## The three modules

- [Content SEO](/content-seo/overview) — AI-written, SEO-optimized blog posts published to your site
- [Local SEO](/local-seo/overview) — Google Business Profile, reviews, and local rankings
- [Social Media](/social-media/overview) — AI-generated posts across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X
