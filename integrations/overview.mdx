theStacc publishes your generated blog posts straight to your website. Connect one of the supported destinations and, depending on your publishing mode, new posts go live (or land as drafts) automatically. Manage every connection under **Content SEO \> Settings \> Publishing**, in the **Destination** section.

## Which option fits my site?

| Your site is on | Use | How it posts |
|---|---|---|
| WordPress.com (you manage it at wordpress.com) | [WordPress.com](/integrations/wordpress-com) | Automatic. Sign in and approve, no password to copy |
| WordPress on your own hosting (Hostinger, GoDaddy, Bluehost, SiteGround...) | [WordPress.org](/integrations/wordpress-org) | Automatic, with an Application Password |
| Webflow | [Webflow](/integrations/webflow) | Automatic, with an API token |
| Shopify | [Shopify](/integrations/shopify) | Automatic. Approve theStacc in Shopify |
| Ghost | [Ghost](/integrations/ghost) | Automatic, with an Admin API key |
| Zepio | [Zepio](/integrations/zepio) | Automatic, with a Developer API key |
| Squarespace | [Squarespace](/integrations/squarespace) | Copy and paste: theStacc gets it ready, you paste it in |
| Wix, Framer or any other site | [Other website](/integrations/other-website) | Copy and paste: theStacc gets it ready, you paste it in |
| Your own system or app | [Custom Webhook](/integrations/custom-webhook) | Automatic, theStacc sends each post to your endpoint |

Not sure what your site runs on? Open your website, right-click, choose **View page source** and search for `wp-content` (WordPress), `squarespace`, `wix`, `webflow` or `shopify`.

## Two rules that apply to every destination

**Only one destination is active at a time.** A project publishes to a single place. When you turn on a new destination, theStacc automatically turns the previous one off — your old connection settings are kept, so you can switch back later without re-entering anything. The Publishing page says it plainly under Destination: *"One destination is active at a time."*

**You need to be a project admin to connect, switch, or test a destination.** Connecting a platform, toggling it on or off, editing its settings, and running a test are all admin-only actions. If you're a member without admin rights, the controls are visible but disabled. (Ask your project owner or an admin to make the change, or to grant you admin on the project.)
