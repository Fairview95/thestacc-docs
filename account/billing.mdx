title: "Billing & Plans"
description: "Review module subscriptions, payment details, invoices, and usage intervals, and resolve billing issues for the selected project."
---

Select your project and open **Workspace > Billing** to review its module subscriptions. Billing is an admin tab in the current interface. If you cannot see it, ask a project administrator to help rather than buying a plan from another account to work around the missing control.

Module cards relate to the selected project; **Payment Method** and **Invoices** show account-level billing information. Check both the project and the subscription identified in the billing portal before making changes.

## Choose a plan

1. In Billing, find the module you want. The cards are labelled **Content**, **Local SEO**, and **Social**; the pricing screen calls them **Blog SEO**, **Local SEO**, and **Social Media**.
2. Use **Upgrade** on an inactive module to open pricing. If no modules are enabled on the project, Billing offers **Get Started** instead.
3. Choose **Billed monthly** or **Billed yearly**, then select the intended content tier. Bundle cards are farther down the same pricing screen.
4. On an available option, **Start Free Trial** opens checkout. Check the actual trial terms rather than treating this label as confirmation that a free trial is available to your account.
5. Read the selected modules, capacity, currency, billing interval, and total before confirming. If these differ from the plan you intended to buy, stop and ask support to check them.

See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing) for module-selection guidance. These are software subscriptions; a managed-service agreement is a separate offer.

## Manage an existing subscription

An active Billing card has a **Manage** button that opens the billing portal. An inactive card has **Upgrade**, which opens pricing. The pricing screen's disabled **Plan Active** or **Trial Active** button is a status indicator, not a management control.

Use the controls that match your task:

- **Manage** on a module: open the portal and identify the subscription you intend to change.
- **Update** under Payment Method: open the portal to review or change the applicable payment method.
- **View all invoices** under Invoices, when shown: open the portal to review billing history and available invoice downloads.

The portal's available actions depend on the subscription and billing settings. A plan-change option is not guaranteed for every product. Dodo Payments describes those controls and their settings in its [customer portal documentation](https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/customer-portal).

### If the portal or billing details do not load

Allow the page to finish loading, then try opening the portal again. If no tab appears, check whether your browser blocked the new tab. If the app reports that the portal is unavailable or that no billing account was found, contact support with your project, affected module, and the exact message. Do not share full payment-card details.

An empty payment-method or invoice section does not, by itself, prove that you have never been charged. Check the portal and any payment confirmation you received; the app's billing-history request can fail without showing a detailed error.

### After a payment change or cancellation

Read the portal's confirmation and applicable date, then return to the app and recheck the module. Updating a payment method does not by itself confirm a successful charge or restored access. If a payment problem remains, use the provider's message and contact support rather than assuming an automatic retry will succeed.

For cancellation, check the subscription and the effective cancellation date in the portal. Do not assume that every trial and paid module remains usable until the same cutoff. [Subscription status & access](/account/subscription-status-access) explains the separate status, date, usage, and permission checks.

**Delete this project** is a separate action in Billing's Danger Zone. It is not a substitute for checking and cancelling a subscription in the portal.

## Content allowances

Check the selected module's plan name, allowance, and reset interval where they are shown. Content cards can describe a blog allowance per month, week, day, or subscription period, depending on the plan information available. A payment date and a content-usage reset date answer different questions.

For example, annual billing tells you how often the subscription is billed. It does not establish whether your particular content allowance resets monthly or by another interval. If generation is blocked, check the usage message and the plan's reset information before deciding that another payment is required.

A post-tier label alone does not explain how every module counts usage. If you cannot find the relevant allowance or reset information, ask support to confirm it for that module and plan. Avoid using another module's allowance as a substitute.

Subscription access also does not complete your publishing setup. Website and social connections, approval settings, and scheduling still need attention. See [Publishing setup](/content-seo/publishing) for website publishing requirements.

## Bundles

Compare bundles below the individual cards on the pricing screen. Check the included modules, entry capacity, billing interval, and checkout amount for the bundle itself.

An existing included module can block a bundle purchase. The pricing card may show **Active** or name modules to cancel first. Review the existing subscriptions and contact support about switching before cancelling them. The bundle checkout does not automatically combine standalone subscriptions or confirm a refund, credit, or uninterrupted transition.

After buying a bundle, review each included module in the selected project's Billing view. If a module is missing or an action is unavailable, keep the checkout confirmation and ask support to check the subscription and project. Repeatedly buying the bundle is not a reliable way to resolve an activation problem.
