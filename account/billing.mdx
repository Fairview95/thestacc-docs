Choose your project and open **Workspace > Billing**. Use **View plans** in the sidebar to compare module capacities and bundles.

## Choose a plan

1. Click **View plans**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Pick **Individual modules** or **Bundles**.
4. For a module, select its monthly content capacity and choose **Review selection**.
5. Read the selected modules, billing interval, total, and any subscription changes before confirming checkout.

See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing) for the current plan comparison and screenshots.

## Manage an existing subscription

Active modules show **Manage plan**. Billing provides the subscription details and billing-portal controls available to your account. Use the portal to update payment details, view invoices, or manage cancellation.

Check the selected project and subscription before making changes. If you cannot manage billing, ask the account owner.

## Content allowances

Your plan includes a content allowance per billing cycle. Yearly payment does not grant all twelve months of content at once. Review the module's remaining allowance before generating more content.

Choosing a plan does not replace connection setup or approval. Connect your website or social accounts and choose the publishing mode you want.

## Bundles

The **Bundles** tab combines the entry tiers of two or three modules with the displayed bundle discount. If an included module is already active, follow the shown management or upgrade flow and review the changes before paying.
