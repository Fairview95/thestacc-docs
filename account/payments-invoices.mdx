Select the correct project, then open **Workspace > Billing**. The page shows module subscriptions, a payment-method summary and recent payment records. If Billing is hidden, ask the project owner or an admin to help.

Payment records and portal access follow the billing customer linked to your signed-in profile. Switching projects alone does not establish that you are looking at another person's payment history. Check with the person responsible for paying before making a change.

## Payment method

The **Payment Method** card can show the returned card brand and masked last digits. It may instead show a generic payment-method summary or **No payment method on file**.

An empty summary does not establish that no payment method exists: an unavailable provider response can also leave the application without details. Recheck the page or ask the billing contact to inspect the portal if the display differs from your records.

### Update your payment method

1. Open **Workspace > Billing**.
2. Click **Update** on the **Payment Method** card.
3. In the billing portal, check the customer and subscription, then follow the available payment-method controls.
4. Read the portal's confirmation and effective payment details. Return to theStacc and refresh the page to recheck the summary.

**Update**, a subscribed module's **Manage** button and **View all invoices** open the same billing portal in a new tab. If the portal cannot open, check that the signed-in profile has a billing customer and contact support if needed. Do not start a new subscription just to resolve a missing card summary.

## Payments history

The section labeled **Invoices** shows recent payment records with a date, amount, currency and status. Successful records appear as **Paid**; other returned states keep their own labels.

The current billing page requests five recent records. This is a recent-payment summary, not proof of a complete invoice history. When records are present, **View all invoices** opens the portal for further billing information.

**No invoices yet** means the page has no records to display. A provider retrieval failure can also produce an empty list, so compare the portal and your payment confirmation before concluding that you have never been charged.

## Invoices

Use the billing portal to check the invoice documents and download controls available for your customer. The current theStacc page displays payment summaries rather than an in-app invoice PDF download.

Do not treat a payment status as confirmation that an invoice document was emailed to a particular person. Check the portal's billing contact and document availability. If you cannot find the invoice you need, contact [hello@thestacc.com](mailto:hello@thestacc.com) with the relevant payment reference and date. Do not send a full card number.

## What else you can do in the billing portal

Check the portal's available controls before changing payment details, billing information or a subscription. Confirm the affected customer, module and effective date, and keep the resulting confirmation. Opening **Manage** does not itself cancel a subscription. Complete the cancellation in the portal and read its effective date.

Cancelling an unpaid trial can end access immediately; paid cancellation can retain access until a recorded period end. See [Subscription Status & Access](/account/subscription-status-access) for the separate access checks.

## Who can manage payments

The current Workspace interface shows its Billing tab to project owners and admins. That does not make every project admin the payer or give them the same billing-customer records. Ask the person responsible for payment to check the correct customer and authorize changes.

If you can see a project but cannot open Billing or find its payment records, review [Workspace & Team](/account/workspace-team) and ask an owner or admin for help.

## Frequently asked

**Why can't I see my full card number?**

The payment summary exposes masked card information rather than a full card number. Use the billing portal for supported payment-method changes. Never paste card details into a support message.

**Where do I download an invoice PDF?**

Open the portal through **View all invoices** when that button is available, or **Update** on the payment-method card. Check its invoice documents and download options. Ask support if the document is missing.

**How do I change the card I'm billed on?**

Use **Update** in **Workspace > Billing**, then follow the portal's available controls. Confirm the customer, subscription and payment-method change before assuming it applies to a future charge.

**How do I change which plan I'm on or start a trial?**

See [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) for the separate subscription and plan-change workflow.
