title: "Trials Explained"
description: "Check trial eligibility, module limits, publishing access and the next charge before starting a software subscription."
---

Before starting a theStacc software trial, check the selected module, any amount due now and the paid subscription that follows it. Trial eligibility and limits matter as much as the advertised duration.

Choose the correct project and open **Workspace > Billing** to inspect its module subscriptions. If the Billing tab is unavailable, ask the project owner or an admin. These software trials are separate from a managed marketing service and its agreed terms.

## The free trial

A free trial, when offered at checkout, lets you evaluate the selected module within its trial allowances. It does not mean every action available on a paid plan is available during the trial.

For **Content SEO**, you can evaluate generated drafts and previews. External website publishing can be held during an unpaid subscription trial until a first payment is recorded. An article appearing in the preview is not confirmation that it was published to your site.

Check the settings and remaining allowance for the module you are evaluating. A trial subscription also does not replace the connection setup or review needed before publishing.

## How long a trial lasts

Use the trial terms presented for your checkout and the end date shown for your subscription. Billing displays a countdown for a recognized trial; a pricing-card label alone does not establish the dates or eligibility of your account.

Prior trial use can affect a later checkout. The eligibility check considers account and website history, so trying another module does not guarantee another free trial. A repeat checkout may start paid billing without a trial. Review the amount due before confirming.

If a trial is shown without a clear end date, or its dates conflict with what you expected, ask support to check the selected project and module rather than assuming access continues indefinitely.

## Choosing your paid plan at checkout

1. Select the project and module you intend to use.
2. Review the available plan and billing interval.
3. Check the checkout summary, including any trial, amount due now and subsequent recurring charge.
4. Complete checkout only when the selection matches your intention.
5. Return to Billing and verify that the correct module has the expected access.

Payment confirmation and module activation can update at different times. If checkout completes but the plan has not appeared, refresh the subscription information and contact support with the project, module and payment reference if the mismatch remains. Do not assume another purchase is needed merely because a card has not refreshed.

See [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) for plan selection and subscription management.

## Subscription status during and after a trial

A trial can appear as **Trial** in Billing. Paid access can appear as **Active**; a cancelled, expired or unavailable subscription needs a separate check. The label is not a complete record of your remaining allowance, permissions or publishing readiness.

Use [Subscription Status & Access](/account/subscription-status-access) to interpret the module, its dates and the action that is unavailable. In particular, a payment problem may result in an inactive module rather than a visible **Past Due** card.

## What happens when a trial ends

### If you do nothing

A subscription trial can lead into the recurring plan selected at checkout. Check the upcoming charge and billing interval before the trial ends. Dodo Payments describes this distinction between [starting a trial and collecting its first payment](https://docs.dodopayments.com/developer-resources/subscription-integration-guide).

Continued access depends on the payment and theStacc's subscription update. Do not treat trial conversion as a guarantee that every scheduled generation or publishing action will complete without interruption.

### If billing does not go through

A payment failure can restrict the module. Open its billing controls, follow the payment instructions and verify the resulting payment and access. Updating a payment method does not guarantee that the next attempt will succeed.

If the portal and theStacc show different states, contact support with the affected module and the payment reference. Avoid sending card numbers or other sensitive payment details in a support message.

### Cancelling before (or after) you are charged

Open Billing for the correct project and use the module's **Manage** control to reach the billing portal. Complete the cancellation in that portal; opening it is not confirmation of cancellation.

**Cancelling an unpaid trial can end module access immediately.** Do not assume you can cancel and continue using the remainder of the trial. For a paid subscription, check the cancellation's effective date and the access available in the module. If paid-period access does not match the confirmation, ask support to investigate.

Cancellation and **Delete project** are different actions. Use the billing portal to manage a subscription. Check already published material at its destination separately; a subscription label does not establish the state of your website or social accounts.

## Local SEO trial specifics

Review the location and post allowances applying to the trial you are offered. Select the business profile you intend to manage, confirm the connection and inspect generated material before publishing.

Do not assume that connecting a location gives it a paid plan's allowance. The [Local SEO Overview](/local-seo/overview) explains the module's workflow; use the selected plan and in-app allowance for your account's limits.

## Social Media trial specifics

Check the trial's post and connected-platform allowances before adding accounts or scheduling content. Review which accounts are connected and which publishing mode is selected.

A generated social post is different from a successful publication to each selected account. Inspect the resulting post and destination rather than treating trial access as proof of delivery. See the [Social Media Overview](/social-media/overview) for the workflow.

## Admin-granted trials

A team-granted evaluation may use different activation rules and dates from self-serve checkout. Some grants have a stated expiry; a trial-style label does not by itself establish either a free checkout trial or indefinite access.

Confirm the modules, limits and end date with the person arranging the evaluation. If the displayed date or access differs from that agreement, ask support to check the grant. A grant should not be interpreted as a new paid-subscription commitment.
