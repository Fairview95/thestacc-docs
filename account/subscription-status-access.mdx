A module subscription is one part of access in theStacc. Its activation, expiry, remaining allowance and your project role also affect which actions you can perform. A billing label alone does not prove that generation or publishing will succeed.

Select the correct project, then open **Workspace > Billing**. Content SEO, Local SEO and Social Media are tracked separately, including when a purchase covers several modules. If Billing is hidden, ask the project owner or an admin to check it.

This page concerns software access. A separately agreed managed marketing service has its own scope and commercial terms.

## The four statuses

The labels below describe common subscription situations, not every possible provider state. An ended or unavailable activation may appear as **No Plan** or **No active subscription** instead.

### Active

The module has an active subscription or granted plan. Generation still depends on its allowance and your permissions; publishing can also need a working connection and the appropriate review.

If an action is blocked despite an Active label, record the exact message and check the selected project, module and allowance. A paid invoice or an Active badge is not a complete diagnosis of that failure.

### Trialing

A recognized trial appears as **Trial** in Billing, with the remaining days and end date when available. It operates within trial allowances, not guaranteed paid-feature parity.

External website publishing can be held during an unpaid Content SEO trial until a first payment is recorded. Use [Trials Explained](/account/trials-explained) to review eligibility, payment transition and cancellation before relying on trial access.

### Past due

A failed payment needs attention. The billing update can restrict access, and a module card may show no active subscription rather than this exact label. Check the payment information and portal instead of waiting for a specific badge.

Update the payment method or complete the action requested by the portal, then check that the payment succeeded and module access returned. Dodo Payments' [payment-failure guidance](https://docs.dodopayments.com/developer-resources/handle-payment-failures) explains that recovery depends on the failure and billing setup. Updating a card does not guarantee a successful charge or immediate restoration.

### Cancelled

Complete cancellation in the billing portal and read its effective date. Opening the portal alone does not cancel the subscription.

Cancelling an unpaid trial can revoke its activation immediately. Paid cancellation can retain access until a recorded period end, but check the module's actual availability rather than assuming every background task continues until that date. Contact support if it differs from the cancellation confirmation.

Managing a subscription is separate from deleting the project. Check material already published to external destinations there; this status is not confirmation of its continued availability.

## Admin-granted plans

A grant may have different activation rules from a self-serve subscription. Some granted plans have an expiry, while other activations are not governed by a trial countdown. Neither a trial-style label nor a missing trial date establishes indefinite access on its own.

Confirm the grant's modules, allowance and end date with the person arranging it. If the display conflicts with that agreement, ask support to inspect the selected project and module. Do not purchase a replacement simply because a trial-style label looks unfamiliar.

## How access works across your team

A project subscription can supply module access to invited teammates without each person purchasing that module individually. Membership and role are separate requirements: being invited does not automatically grant every editing, publishing or billing action.

Check that the teammate joined the intended project and has the role needed for the task. Billing is an admin concern in the current workspace interface. See [Workspace & Team](/account/workspace-team) for member management and roles.

## Your plan limit and how it resets

Keep the billing interval separate from the usage-reset interval. A yearly payment does not establish a pooled annual allowance. The plan's configured reset interval determines the usage window; Billing can show an allowance as blogs per month, week, day or period when that information is available.

Read the allowance and current usage for the module you are working in. A generation limit is different from an expired subscription or a permission error. Check the stated reset information before choosing whether to wait, change the plan or ask for help.

Use [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) for plan management. Do not infer another module's allowance or reset date from a Content SEO card.

## Where to check your plan and status

1. Select the correct project.
2. Open **Workspace > Billing**, or ask an owner or admin to check it.
3. Find the affected module and review its plan, dates and available allowance.
4. Compare those details with the exact message from the blocked action.
5. If the information conflicts, contact support with the project, module, relevant date and payment reference where applicable.

After checkout, refresh the subscription information and verify access. Payment confirmation and the application's update need not appear simultaneously. Avoid sending sensitive card details or assuming a repeated checkout is the right fix.
