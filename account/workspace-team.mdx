## Invite a teammate

1. Click **Invite member**.
2. Enter the person's email under **Invite people**.
3. Choose an **Invitation role**: Admin, Editor, or Viewer.
4. Click **Invite**. The person accepts the invitation to join.

<Frame caption="Choose a role before inviting someone.">
  <img src="/images/product/workspace-invite.jpg" alt="Invite to Linear dialog with email entry and Admin, Editor, and Viewer roles" />
</Frame>

Owners and project admins manage invitations and member access. If these controls are unavailable, ask an owner or admin to make the change.

## Choose a role

| Role | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The person responsible for the account and its access |
| **Admin** | Teammates who manage the project, settings, and members |
| **Editor** | Teammates who create, edit, review, and publish content |
| **Viewer** | People who need to read content and settings |

For a member with editable project access, use the role dropdown to change their role or their **Actions** menu to manage access. Account-level access may appear as a fixed badge; manage it at the account level. Your own role is not editable here.

## Invite with a link

Open **Invite member**, then use **General access**. Choose an **Invite link role** and click **Create link**. Share the resulting link only with the people who should join at that role. An Admin link grants member-management access.

Use the link controls to disable or replace a link that should no longer be used. Disabling a link stops future joins; it does not remove people who already joined.

## Switch projects and manage billing

Use the project selector at the top of the sidebar to switch businesses. Check the project name before inviting anyone. Open **Workspace > Billing** for subscriptions and invoices, or **View plans** to compare plans. See [Billing & Plans](/account/billing).
