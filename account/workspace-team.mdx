title: "Workspace & Team"
description: "Invite project teammates, choose roles, review pending invitations and manage invite links without confusing account and project access."
---

Select the correct project, then open **Workspace > Members**. This page lists people with access to that project and its current pending invitations. Check the project name before inviting someone or changing a role.

## Invite a teammate

1. Click **Invite** on the Members page.
2. Choose **Admin**, **Editor** or **Viewer** under **Role**.
3. Enter the recipient's **Email Address**, then click **Send Invitation**.
4. Check the pending invitation in the member list. The recipient must sign in with the invited email address and accept the invitation.

An invitation is not the same as joined membership. If the recipient cannot find the email, check the address and ask them to check their inbox and spam folder. An invitation can be created even when email delivery fails; the success message alone does not establish that it arrived.

Owners and project admins can manage invitations. Use **Resend invitation** for an outstanding invitation, or cancel it if the address or access request is wrong. If the invitation has expired, send a fresh invitation. If **Already invited** appears, use the available resend action rather than repeatedly submitting the same request.

If invite controls are missing, ask the project owner or an admin to check your role and the selected project.

## Choose a role

Choose the lowest role that supports the teammate's work. Account access and a role granted for one project are different: an account-level badge cannot be edited through this project's role dropdown.

For an editable project member, use the role dropdown to change their role. Use **Remove from project** and confirm the removal when they no longer need that access. Your own role is not editable here. Ask the account owner about an account-level access change instead of trying to remove its fixed-badge row.

Wait for the success message, then check the member list again. If a change fails, keep the existing access in mind until you have confirmed the result. The application can also reject removal or demotion of the last project admin.

## Invite with a link

The **Invite link** card is on the Members page for owners and project admins. Choose a role and click **Create link**. Copy the resulting link and share it only with the intended collaborators.

New collaborators can sign in and use a working invite link to join at its selected role. The link does not change an existing member’s role. An **Admin** link grants member-management powers, so choosing that role asks for confirmation. A share link is not restricted to one recipient's email address in the way an email invitation is.

Use **Regenerate** to replace the current link or **Disable link** to stop new joins through it. Changing the link's role also replaces the link, so copy the current URL afterward. These changes do not remove people who already joined; review their access separately in the member list.

## Switch projects and manage billing

Use the project selector to switch businesses, then check the Members list for the selected project. A project invitation does not grant access to every business in an account.

Owners and project admins can see **Workspace > Billing** in the current interface. Billing access and the customer paying a subscription are separate considerations. Use [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) for subscription controls and [Payments, Invoices & Payment Methods](/account/payments-invoices) for payment records and the billing portal.
