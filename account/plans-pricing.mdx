Choose a software plan around the workflow you need: website articles, Google Business Profile content, or social posts. Select your project, then open **Workspace > Billing**. An inactive module's **Upgrade** button opens the plan-selection screen. Compare the selected capacity and billing interval before starting checkout.

These instructions cover theStacc's self-serve software subscriptions. A separately agreed managed-service proposal has its own scope and billing terms.

## Individual modules

The pricing screen has separate cards for **Blog SEO**, **Local SEO**, and **Social Media**. Each card lists its content tiers and advertised features. Select a tier on an available module rather than assuming that every card's highlighted option is the plan you want.

A content tier describes capacity, not a promise that every item will be generated or published immediately. Connection setup, approval settings, and scheduling remain separate tasks. For website publishing, see [Publishing setup](/content-seo/publishing).

An active module has disabled tier controls and shows **Plan Active** or **Trial Active** on the pricing screen. Return to Billing to manage that subscription. Buying a second copy of the same module is not the way to change an existing plan.

## Bundles

Bundle cards appear below the individual modules. **Content + Social** combines Blog SEO and Social Media; **Content + Local SEO** combines Blog SEO and Local SEO; **Local SEO + Social** combines those modules; **All-in-One** includes all of them. Check the included-module labels and the bundle's displayed entry capacity before choosing it.

Selecting a higher individual tier does not customize a bundle's capacity. Compare the bundle card itself with the individual tier you need.

If all included modules are active, the bundle button shows **Active**. If only some are active, it is disabled and identifies the modules to cancel first. Review those subscriptions in Billing and contact support about switching before cancelling a service you still need. The bundle checkout does not automatically merge an existing standalone subscription into a bundle.

## Monthly or yearly

Use the **Billed yearly** / **Billed monthly** control to change the billing interval. Check its current state before selecting a plan; do not assume the screen opens with monthly billing selected.

For yearly billing, the screen shows an annual headline amount and a monthly equivalent. A monthly equivalent helps compare costs; it is not a promise that you will be charged monthly. At checkout, read the currency, total, billing interval, trial terms if offered, and any tax or discount information shown for your selection.

Keep the billing interval separate from the content-usage reset interval. Check the selected plan's allowance and reset information rather than multiplying a displayed monthly capacity into a promised annual pool. [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) explains how to review those fields.

If the plan card and checkout disagree about the amount, interval, or included modules, stop before confirming and ask support to check the selection. A saved screenshot or a previous plan's price is not a current quote.

## Review and manage a plan

On an available module or bundle, **Start Free Trial** opens checkout directly. The button label does not establish eligibility for another free trial. Read the selected checkout's terms, especially when you have used theStacc before. See [Trials explained](/account/trials-explained).

Before confirming, check that you are buying for the intended project, module or bundle, capacity, and billing interval. If checkout reports an existing subscription or an unavailable plan, review Billing instead of repeatedly trying another purchase.

After checkout, return to **Workspace > Billing** and check the relevant module. Payment confirmation and the app's subscription update can appear at different times. If the expected plan remains unavailable, keep the checkout confirmation and ask support to check the project and subscription. Do not make another purchase solely because the card has not updated.

Use **Manage** on an active Billing card to open the billing portal. Review the subscription identified there before changing payment details, cancelling, or using any available plan-change controls.
