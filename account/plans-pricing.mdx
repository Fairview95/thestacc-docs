## Bundles

Bundles combine the entry tier of each included module and show a 15% saving compared with buying them separately.

| Bundle | Included modules | Listed monthly price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **All-in-One** | Blog SEO + Local SEO + Social Media | \$167 |
| **Content + Social** | Blog SEO + Social Media | \$126 |
| **Content + Local SEO** | Blog SEO + Local SEO | \$126 |
| **Local SEO + Social** | Local SEO + Social Media | \$83 |

If you already have an included module, use the shown **Manage plans** or upgrade flow. Review your existing subscriptions in Billing before switching; avoid buying an overlapping plan.

## Monthly or yearly

**Yearly** shows a **Save 17%** label and a monthly equivalent, with the amount charged once per year underneath. For example, the 30-article Blog SEO tier shows \$82.50 per month, billed as \$990 once per year. The 30-post Local SEO and Social Media tiers each show \$40.83 per month, billed as \$490 once per year. Read the billing amount and interval for the selected option before paying. Yearly billing does not make the entire year's content allowance available at once; content capacity still refreshes by cycle.

## Review and manage a plan

Choose **Review selection** on an available module to check what you are buying before checkout. An active module shows **Manage plan**. Manage subscriptions, payment details, and invoices under **Workspace > Billing**. See [Billing & Plans](/account/billing).
