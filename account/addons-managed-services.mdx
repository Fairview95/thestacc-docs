Most of theStacc is self-service software: AI does the work, you review and approve. But you have two ways to go further. **Add-ons** stack extra capacity onto a plan you already have. **Managed services** hand the entire job to our team, so you do not have to touch the dashboard at all.

This guide covers both.

## Add-ons

Add-ons are optional extras you can attach to a module subscription. They are billed monthly, on top of your base plan, and you can stack more than one. Add-ons are added to your cart at checkout, right next to your base plan - so you pay for everything in one place, on one card.

There is no separate checkout for an add-on by itself. An add-on always rides along with a base plan for the same module.

### Content SEO add-ons

If 30 articles a month is not enough, these add more:

- **Growth Pack - +$50/month** - adds 20 more articles per month, for 50 total.
- **Scale Pack - +$100/month** - adds 50 more articles per month, for 80 total.

The extra articles go through the same keyword research, planning, SEO scoring, and publishing flow as your base articles. Nothing else about your setup changes.

### Local SEO add-ons

- **Extra Posts - +$30/month** - adds 30 more Google Business Profile posts per month.
- **Extra Keywords - +$10/month** - adds 10 more local keyword tracking slots so you can watch more search terms.
- **Priority Support - +$50/month** - a dedicated support line with priority response times.

### How to add an add-on

Add-ons are arranged through our team rather than a self-serve toggle in the pricing page. If you want one:

1. Make sure you already have (or are starting) a base plan for the matching module.
2. Reach out to **[hello@thestacc.com](mailto:hello@thestacc.com)** for how subscriptions, trials, and cancellation work.

## Managed services

Managed services are the opposite of self-service. Instead of you reviewing and approving content, **our team runs everything for you**. We hold the logins, do the work every day, and report back. AI does the heavy lifting on content and posts; a dedicated human manager handles quality checks, strategy, and your monthly check-in call.

This is the right fit if you would rather not log in at all - you just want results, a report, and someone to talk to once a month.

Managed services are not a self-serve checkout. Every tier starts with a conversation, because we need access to your accounts and we tailor the work to your business. Use the **Talk to Our Team** button on the pricing page, or email **[hello@thestacc.com](mailto:hello@thestacc.com)**.

Every managed tier is built on three things working together:

- **AI does the volume.** The same engines behind our software modules generate your blog articles, Google Business Profile posts, and social content at scale.
- **A human manager owns quality.** A dedicated manager reviews the output, fixes anything off, sets the strategy, and makes sure what goes live actually fits your business and voice.
- **A monthly strategy call.** You and your manager meet once a month to review performance and plan what is next.

You get a read-only view of what is happening, regular reports, and a real person to reach - without writing a word or approving a single post yourself.

## Add-on vs managed service - which do I want?

- **Pick an add-on** if you are happy running the software yourself and just need more volume (more articles, posts, or keywords) or faster support. You stay in control; you just get more of what your plan already does.
- **Pick a managed service** if you would rather not log in at all. We hold the logins, do the work daily, run quality control, and meet you once a month.
