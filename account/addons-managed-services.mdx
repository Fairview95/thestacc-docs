Choose extra software capacity when your team can still review and manage the work. Choose managed support when you need someone to handle agreed delivery tasks. Before buying either, confirm what your current plan includes and who will check business facts and approve publication.

## Add-ons

An add-on changes the scope or capacity of a software subscription. It does not transfer responsibility for reviewing content to a service team. Compare the available software tiers first: a higher-capacity tier and an add-on are different purchases.

Confirm the price, matching module, billing period and activation date before placing an order. Do not assume extra capacity has become available merely because you requested it.

### Content SEO add-ons

Ask the team about Growth Pack or Scale Pack if your current article allowance does not cover your planned work. Confirm the additional allowance and how it will be counted for your selected subscription.

Keep the request separate from the editorial decision. More capacity is useful when you have distinct reader questions to answer; it does not justify duplicate topics or articles that need no further detail. See [Content Plans & Keywords](/content-seo/content-plans) before expanding the plan.

### Local SEO add-ons

For Extra Posts, confirm the number of additional Google Business Profile posts and which location they cover. For Extra Keywords, confirm the additional tracking slots. For Priority Support, agree the contact channel and response expectations.

These requests serve different needs. Posting capacity does not increase tracking slots, and priority support should not be treated as a promise to resolve every issue immediately.

### How to add an add-on

1. Check the selected project, active module and current capacity.
2. Describe the extra work you need to [hello@thestacc.com](mailto:hello@thestacc.com).
3. Ask for the price, subscription compatibility, billing period and activation instructions in writing.
4. Check the resulting allowance and charge after the change is confirmed.

Do not start another subscription checkout to change an existing plan unless the team confirms that route. See [Billing & Plans](/account/billing) for the separate plan-change workflow.

## Managed services

Managed delivery covers agreed work performed for your business. Growth Complete includes a managed website, search and AI-search content, Google Business Profile work, review response and social distribution.

Agree who provides business information, checks drafts, approves publication and handles account access. Managed support does not make an unsupported claim accurate or remove review responsibilities that your business needs to retain.

Confirm the full proposal before committing. Specify the locations, channels, delivery volume, reporting, website maintenance and exclusions. Ask the team to confirm availability and terms for any other advertised managed tier; do not infer its scope from Growth Complete.

### How the price compares

Compare the same work across options. Include setup, writing, factual review, publishing, account management and website maintenance, as well as the time your team will still spend.

A quoted service fee and a software subscription do not cover the same responsibilities. Ask each provider to identify its deliverables, exclusions, ownership and cancellation terms. Use your actual quotes rather than an assumed agency or salary benchmark.

### Pricing for customers in India

The Growth Complete amounts above are in US dollars. Ask the team for a written rupee quote if that is how you need to pay. Confirm the total, billing period and applicable charges; do not convert a dollar price yourself and assume it is an available checkout offer.

## Add-on vs managed service - which do I want?

Choose an add-on or a higher-capacity software tier if you can manage the workflow and need more agreed capacity. Choose managed delivery if you need help executing defined tasks. In both cases, decide who checks the content and who authorizes publication.
