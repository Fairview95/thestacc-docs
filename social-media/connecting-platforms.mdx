Choose your project, then open **Connections** under **Social** in the sidebar. The **Social publishing** page brings account connections, publishing modes, and posting schedules together.

## Connect an account

1. Click **Connect Instagram**, **Connect Facebook**, **Connect LinkedIn**, or the X connection button.
2. Read the platform's requirements, then continue to its authorization screen.
3. Sign in to the platform and approve access to the account or page you want to use.
4. Return to theStacc and check that the correct account is **Connected**. Use **Refresh** if the status has not updated.

| Platform | Account requirements shown in the app |
| --- | --- |
| **Instagram** | Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page; personal accounts are not supported |
| **Facebook** | A Facebook Page with Admin or Editor access; personal profiles are not supported |
| **LinkedIn** | A personal profile or Company Page; Company Pages require Super Admin access |
| **X/Twitter** | The X account you want to publish from |

Connections belong to the selected project. Use **Connect another account** where available to add another destination. Choose the default account, or enable **Publish posts here** for additional Facebook and LinkedIn pages you want to target.

## Choose how posts go out

Use **Publishing mode** to choose a mode for each connected platform. A disconnected platform's mode control stays unavailable until it has an account.

Review the account name and selected publishing mode before turning on automation. See [Posting schedules & timezones](/social-media/content-scheduling).

## Fix a connection

If an account is expired or cannot publish, use the reconnect flow, complete the platform authorization, then refresh its status. Check that you granted access to the intended page or organization. **Disconnect** removes the connection from this project; it does not delete the platform account.

When a publish fails, read the result for each platform before retrying. See [Post states & failures](/social-media/post-states-failures).
