Open **Social > Connections**. Under **Posting schedule**, pick the account you want to configure. Each connected account can have its own timezone and weekly posting times.

## Set the timezone

1. Choose the account from **Account posting schedule**.
2. Click its **Timezone** picker.
3. Search for a city and select the timezone for that account's intended audience.
4. Review the weekly times, then click **Save schedule** after making changes.

<Frame caption="Search by city to choose the account's local timezone.">

All times in this weekly schedule use the timezone shown above it. For example, **10:00 AM** with **Kolkata (GMT+5:30)** means 10 AM in India, not 10 AM UTC or your computer's local time. Choose a named city timezone so seasonal offset changes can be handled where applicable.

## Recommended times

Click **Use recommended times** to fill the week's suggested times, then **Save schedule** to apply them.

| Platform | Recommended local time |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | 12:00 PM |
| Facebook | 12:00 PM |
| LinkedIn | 4:00 PM |
| X/Twitter | 10:00 AM |

These are theStacc's current default starting points, not a guarantee of the best time for every audience. Begin with them, compare engagement in your analytics, and adjust for the people you want to reach.

The recommended schedule enables every day with one suggested time. It supplies available posting slots; your content plan and cadence still determine which posts are created. A seven-day schedule does not create seven posts automatically.

## Customize days and times

- Turn a day's switch off to skip that day.
- Click a posting time to change it.
- Use **Add posting time for**, choose a day or **Every day**, select a new time, then click **Add time**.
- Remove a time with its remove control.
- Click **Save schedule**. Use **Cancel changes** to discard an unsaved schedule.

Keep at least one enabled posting time. To pause automatic posting, choose **Manual** in the platform's publishing mode instead of saving an empty schedule.

## What changes affect

Saved changes apply to **new automatic posts**. Posts already sent to the publisher keep their current schedule. Review existing scheduled posts individually when changing timezones or posting times.

For a manually scheduled post, check the date, time, and timezone displayed in that post's scheduling controls before confirming. See [Publishing & scheduling](/social-media/publishing-scheduling).
