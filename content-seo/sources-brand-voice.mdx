Sources & Brand Voice separates factual material from writing samples for your selected project. Upload or paste facts under **Source material**, analyze your own writing under **Brand voice**, and review both before generating. The saved inputs guide generation; you still need to check the draft for accuracy and the intended tone.

Open **Business Info** in the sidebar, then choose **Sources**. The shared view has **Source material**, **Brand voice** and **Brand images** tabs. Editing needs project-editor access. Uploads or analysis can be blocked by project access or subscription checks. If either action fails, read the displayed error before retrying.

## Source material

Choose **Source material**, then use **Upload files** or **Paste text**. The shared view's file control lists **PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown**, up to **50 MB per file**. Use readable text; a file extension alone does not confirm that its contents can be extracted.

For pasted material, enter the text and an optional title, then click **Add text**. Surrounding whitespace is trimmed. Use a title that lets you recognize the material later. Uploading or adding text saves that source separately from the **Business info** tab's **Save changes** action.

After adding a source:

1. Open its card under **Your sources** to view the extracted text.
2. Check names, amounts, dates and any tables against the original material.
3. If the transcript is empty or incomplete, use readable text instead of assuming the upload succeeded fully.
4. Remove outdated material with the card's × control, then add the corrected source.

The first source card is marked **Primary**. The writer is instructed to prioritize eligible factual sources, with the first included source given priority. Keep that material current and resolve contradictions before generating. A saved card or its badge does not prove that its text reached a particular draft.

Long documents and the combined material sent to the writer can be shortened or omitted to fit the prompt. Check any important claim in the finished draft against its source. Uploading a complete document does not mean every passage was used.

**Illustrative workflow:** Put an approved service description under Source material and one of your own articles under Brand voice. This separates the facts you want referenced from the style you want analyzed. It is an example of organizing inputs, not a generated result or a customer story.

## Brand voice

Choose **Brand voice** and add your own writing under **Your samples**, using the upload or paste controls. Use at least one sample with **100 or more characters after trimming**. A short snippet can be saved in the list while still being too short for voice analysis.

Use paragraphs or articles that represent how you want to write now. Writing samples are used to derive style guidance; factual material belongs under Source material.

1. Add suitable writing samples.
2. Click **Analyze my writing**. The button is unavailable until the view has a sample.
3. Wait for **Voice profile updated**, then read the profile.
4. Check the displayed **Tone**, **Vocabulary**, **Signature patterns**, **Words they use** and **Words they avoid**, where present.
5. After changing the sample set, use **Re-analyze** to update the profile.

Adding or removing a sample changes the sample list; it does not automatically re-analyze the saved profile. If analysis fails, review the error and the available sample text. If the profile cannot load, use **Retry** rather than treating an empty view as confirmation that no profile is saved.

The profile gives the writer instructions about tone, vocabulary and recurring patterns. It is instructed to favor the analyzed voice over the generic writing-style choice in [Preferences](/content-seo/preferences) when they conflict. This is guidance to the model, so check the resulting draft for the intended wording and tone.

Use **Clear** to clear the profile while retaining your writing samples. Wait for **Voice profile cleared**, then check the view again. Clearing or changing a profile does not rewrite existing articles. Review future drafts rather than assuming the profile controls every sentence.

## Photos, videos, and product images

For blog photos, screenshots or diagrams, the Sources view also has **Brand images**. Review their descriptions before using them as writing context or visual inputs. See [Image Generation](/content-seo/image-generation) for checking images in a draft.

Use [Library](/getting-started/library) for the broader media workflow. Source documents and writing samples stay in their respective Sources tabs.
