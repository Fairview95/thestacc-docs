Open **Business Info** in the sidebar, then choose **Sources**. Add reference material for facts and writing samples for your brand voice.

## Source material

Choose **Source material**, then add what your content should draw from:

- **Upload files:** Add PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown documents, up to 50 MB each.
- **Add link:** Add a website page to import reference material.
- **Paste text:** Add product details, notes, a brief, or other information, with a title you can recognize later.

Open a saved source to check its extracted text. Keep the material accurate and remove outdated sources. Use the **Use in content** controls to choose which content types should use it.

Review generated content before publishing. Reference material helps ground the writing, but you should still check names, figures, and claims.

## Brand voice

Choose **Brand voice** and add examples of your own writing. Use complete paragraphs or articles that represent the tone you want.

Click **Analyze my writing**, then review the resulting profile. Add newer samples and use **Re-analyze** when your style changes. **Clear** removes the profile while keeping your samples.

Writing samples guide tone and style. Add factual information under **Source material** instead.

## Photos, videos, and product images

Open [Library](/getting-started/library) in the sidebar to manage your media. Source documents and brand voice stay under **Business Info > Sources**.
