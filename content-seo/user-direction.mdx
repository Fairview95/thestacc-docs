User Direction lets you save writing instructions for one blog before generating its content. Use it to specify the audience, emphasis or details to avoid. Save the instruction before starting generation, then check the resulting draft: a saved instruction guides the AI, but does not guarantee that every request appears exactly as intended.

## Where to find it

Open a blog in **Content SEO**. On its detail page, look for **Your instructions for this post**, marked **Optional**. The current editor shows this field to project editors when the plan is ready and the blog has no content. It is hidden while generation is running and after content exists. A rejected status alone does not determine whether it appears.

The instruction stays in that blog's saved generation settings. Hiding the field after generation does not clear it; the writer can reuse the saved direction on a later generation.

## How to use it

Save User Direction on the blog detail page before starting generation. Confirm the planned topic, enter a focused request, click **Save** and wait for the confirmation. Review the generated draft against that request before publishing. Use **Cancel** if you want to restore the last saved instruction.

1. Open the correct blog and confirm its title, keyword and planned angle.
2. Enter a focused instruction in **Your instructions for this post**.
3. Click **Save** and wait for the **Saved** confirmation.
4. Start generation using the available blog action.
5. Review the draft against your instruction before approving or publishing it.

Typing does not save the direction. **Cancel** restores the last saved value. The editor trims surrounding whitespace when saving; Save and Cancel appear when the text differs from the saved instruction after trimming. If saving fails, resolve the displayed error before generating.

## What it actually does

During blog generation, the writer receives the saved text in an **Additional Direction from User** prompt section. The writing instructions tell the AI to give this direction priority over its default angle and the earlier excerpt, while retaining content-integrity rules. This is a prompt instruction, so review the output rather than assuming the model followed it perfectly.

Direction can ask the writer to change the audience, examples or emphasis, or to omit a subject. Saving it does not rewrite an article that already exists. It also does not change other posts or your project-wide preferences. Use [Sources & Brand Voice](/content-seo/sources-brand-voice) for supporting material and recurring brand guidance.

## The one thing it can't override

User Direction does not authorize invented facts. The system prompt tells the writer not to fabricate statistics, testimonials, certifications, partnerships or promotions. Supply relevant evidence for any factual detail you want included, and check that the finished article supports it.

Promotion handling needs particular care. One system instruction allows specific promotional details from verified business context, while a separate user-prompt rule prohibits specific discount percentages. User Direction is therefore not a reliable way to insert an exact discount or code, even when you provide it. Review promotional wording manually and ask support about a conflict before publishing.

The [Content Safety & Integrity Guardrails](/content-seo/content-safety-guardrails) guide explains the safeguards and their limits. These instructions help constrain generation; they do not certify that a draft is factually correct or meets your professional obligations.

## Clearing your direction

Delete the field's contents and click **Save** to store an empty direction. The saved field then supplies no special per-post guidance. The generation executor has a separate fallback for direction supplied through another request path, so clearing the field should not be treated as removing every possible instruction from every generation path.

## Length limit

The editor displays a **2,000-character** limit and a live counter. Its textarea prevents further input at that limit. The save endpoint trims surrounding whitespace and keeps at most 2,000 characters; the generator also trims and caps a nonempty saved direction before passing it to the writer.

Keep your main request near the beginning and check the saved text after pasting. Character counting can differ for some characters between the browser and backend, so a message near the limit deserves a quick review. The limit applies to the saved direction, not to the length of the resulting article.

## A few tips

Ask for a clear audience and a specific emphasis rather than stacking unrelated requests. If you mention a customer or partnership, provide the evidence and permission needed to use that detail. A name in an instruction is not proof of the relationship.

Use the field for one-off guidance. Keep recurring voice rules and factual source material in your project settings. After generation, check the introduction, examples and call to action against the instruction; edit any missed request before the article goes live.
