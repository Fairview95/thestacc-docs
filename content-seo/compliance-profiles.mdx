A **compliance profile** adds project-specific disclosure instructions and content checks for regulated businesses. It is **opt-in and off by default**. An enabled profile can help identify missing information or prohibited wording, but it does not determine all the rules that apply to your business or certify a post's compliance.

Have the responsible professional or compliance reviewer confirm the applicable requirements, supplied identifiers, and final content. The setting, an automated verdict, and a publishing approval are different parts of that process.

## Who needs this

The documented profile options cover:

- **Real estate:** agents and brokerages.
- **Mortgage:** loan originators and lenders.
- **Contractor:** licensed trades and general contractors.
- **Healthcare:** providers, clinics, and practices.
- **Law firm:** attorneys.
- **Financial adviser:** advisers and investment firms.
- **Insurance:** agencies and producers.

These are product categories, not a legal assessment of your obligations. Do not infer that a business outside this list has no advertising requirements. Ask the person responsible for compliance whether the available profile matches the work you publish.

## Where to set it up

Open **Settings > Project > Compliance**, shown with a **Beta** badge in the documented interface. The setting is per project, so check the selected project before making changes.

Editing requires **project admin** access. Non-admins can view the saved settings but cannot change them. A permission to edit the profile is not evidence of professional qualification to approve regulated content.

## Setting up a profile

1. Turn on **Enable compliance guardrails**. Until it is enabled, the profile does not apply to generation.
2. Choose the industry. **Financial adviser** and **Insurance** are marked **Advanced**. **Not regulated** enables the documented baseline rules without a vertical-specific profile.
3. Select the primary US state or **Nationwide**. This chooses the product's rule configuration. It does not establish that state-specific obligations cease to apply when you select Nationwide, or that all applicable requirements are represented.
4. Enter the relevant license identifiers. The documented form labels include DRE # for real estate, NMLS # for mortgage, CSLB # for contractors, Board / license # for healthcare, Bar # for law firms, CRD # for financial advisers, and NPN for insurance. Confirm the correct identifier for your actual business and jurisdiction; these labels are not universal licensing instructions.
5. Enter the **responsible firm** or practice name.
6. Add a **custom disclaimer**, if needed. Have its wording reviewed rather than assuming any disclaimer cures an unsupported claim.
7. Click **Save changes**. Admins have the Save control. The **What we'll enforce** panel previews the selected profile's required and prohibited rules.

The documented behavior reports a missing identifier when a disclosure cannot be filled. Review that result and supply real information. Neither an empty field nor an automatically filled disclosure establishes that the legal requirement has been satisfied.

### Apply to content you already have

After saving an enabled profile, admins can use **Apply to existing content > Re-scan existing**. The scan uses saved settings, so save changes first. It can add disclosures where the required details are available and flag drafts for review.

A scan may be partial on a large project. Inspect the reported coverage and run it again if necessary. Do not treat a partial scan, or a scan with no detected issue, as expert approval of the full content library.

## What gets enforced

The documentation describes two layers:

**Writing and disclosure instructions.** The selected profile adds relevant disclosure language and business details to planning and regeneration. Some disclosures can also be appended during publishing when the necessary data is available. Verify their wording, placement, and applicability in the actual output.

**Content restrictions.** The profile steers writing away from the claims it lists as prohibited, such as unsupported guarantees, fabricated testimonials, or unsubstantiated superiority claims. The rule preview depends on the selected vertical and jurisdiction. Read it before relying on it, and review claims against the requirements your professional reviewer identifies.

The profile's intended restrictions are not proof that a draft cannot contain an error. An instruction to avoid patient information, invented credentials, or unsupported outcomes does not replace checking the completed content.

Inspect the listed **violations** and **missing disclosures**. The documentation describes badges on affected drafts and a compliance audit trail recording rule versions, inserted language, missing items, and verdicts. Review these records rather than assuming an absent badge proves that every requirement was checked.

## What happens at publish

The documented gate runs again at publishing:

- **Manual publish:** Block prevents publication; Hold for review can require a human override; None follows the normal publish workflow.
- **Automatic publish:** a block or hold routes the post for review. Some configured profiles require manual review regardless of the verdict. Check the selected profile rather than treating this product setting as a statement of law for every state.

The documented failure paths differ. It is inaccurate to describe both as fail-safe:

A missing or disabled profile does not become enabled automatically. If you need review before publication, confirm the actual [publishing mode](/content-seo/publishing-modes) and responsible reviewer. The unavailable-layer path is not evidence that every remaining disclosure or review control succeeded.

## Overrides: accepting responsibility

The documented manual **Hold for review** override records that someone chose to publish despite a finding. It can include the reason and the person who acted in the audit trail. An override is a workflow decision, not proof that the concern was legally resolved or that the person is appropriately licensed.

The documentation restricts this action to a signed-in person, excludes automated callers such as agent-key integrations, and retains override evidence when a draft is regenerated or re-checked. Confirm the recorded identity and reason before relying on that record. A hard **Block** remains non-overridable in the documented flow and requires content changes.

## Auto-suggestion

Business information may trigger a suggestion to enable a profile and pre-select a likely vertical. The documented behavior is a suggestion only: an admin must enable and save the profile. Dismissing a banner or leaving the profile disabled does not establish that the business is unregulated.

## A note on placeholder language

The documentation identifies some disclosure text with `[PENDING COUNSEL]`, indicating unfinished wording. It describes routing drafts that depend on this text to review instead of automatic publication. Do not publish the placeholder itself or treat the existence of a hold as counsel approval. Obtain appropriate reviewed wording and check the final output.
