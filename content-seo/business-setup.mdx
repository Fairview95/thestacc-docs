## Review your business

In the **Business info** tab, work through these sections:

| Section | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| **Business details** | Business name, website, category, business type, and description |
| **Offering** | The products, services, and features you want content to describe |
| **Audience** | Who you serve and the problems they want to solve |
| **Brand** | How your business should sound and present itself |
| **Competitors** | Businesses you want theStacc to consider when planning content |
| **Locations** | The places your business serves |

Make your changes, then click **Save changes**. Wait for **All changes saved** before leaving. These settings belong to the selected project.

## Refresh your website information

Click **Re-scan website** when your website or offering changes. Review the resulting information and correct anything that does not match your business.

## Add source material

Open the **Sources** tab for supporting documents, links, pasted notes, and brand voice. These references guide new content; they are not published as posts. See [Sources & Brand Voice](/content-seo/sources-brand-voice).

Use [Library](/getting-started/library) for business photos, videos, and product images. Open **AI memory** to review the preferences and context saved for your business.
