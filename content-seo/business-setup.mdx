Choose your project, then open **Business Info** in the sidebar. Use this shared page to check the facts theStacc uses as context for your business. Changes here stay in the form until you click **Save changes**.

The sidebar entry is shown to project editors, admins and owners when a business module is enabled. If you cannot find it, ask the project owner to check your access and module setup.

## Review your business

In the **Business info** tab, check each section against what your business actually offers. Keep audience names specific, and list only locations you serve.

Use **Add** to place a new item in a list and **Remove** to take one out. Then click **Save changes** for the page. Wait for **Business info saved**. Adding a row alone does not save it.

If saving fails, keep the page open and try again. After a connection problem, reopen the page once you have saved to confirm the intended values. A changed form is not proof that the saved profile changed.

For a logo, **Auto-fetch from website** places a candidate in the form for review. Check it, then save; fetching the logo does not save it automatically.

See [Competitors & Audiences](/content-seo/competitors-audiences) for the list controls and the different save behavior in the tabbed Content SEO view.

## Refresh your website information

Use **Re-scan website** after your website changes. Save useful edits first: if the form has unsaved changes, the re-scan asks whether to replace them.

When the scan finishes, review Business Info again. Check your description, offering, audience and competitors before generating new content. Do not assume a successful scan preserved every manual correction. If the scan reports a failure or timeout, check the current saved information before trying again.

## Add source material

Open **Sources**, then choose **Source material** for supporting documents or pasted factual notes. Use **Brand voice** for writing samples. See [Sources & Brand Voice](/content-seo/sources-brand-voice) for that separate workflow.

Use [Library](/getting-started/library) for business photos, videos and product images. Review generated drafts against your current facts before publishing.
