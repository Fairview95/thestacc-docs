Open **Business Info** in the sidebar for the selected project. **Competitors** sits under **Market presence**; **Target audiences** has its own section. Both lists use the page's **Save changes** button.

You may also encounter the tabbed **Content SEO** business-information view. Its **Audience** and **Competitors** tabs have different controls and saving rules. Use the instructions that match the page you see.

Changes in the tabbed view do not necessarily update the shared Business Info profile. Before generating content, reopen the shared page and check the audience and competitor information you intend to use.

## Competitors

List alternatives your customers actually consider. The saved competitor information can provide context for blog generation; entering a competitor is not evidence that its keyword rankings have been retrieved.

### Adding a competitor

In shared **Business Info**, type a name in **Add a competitor**, then press **Enter** or click **Add**. The row appears in the form. Click **Save changes** to persist it.

In the tabbed **Competitors** view, add a website instead. That form adds `https://` when needed and has a **10-competitor** limit. This limit belongs to that view; the shared list uses a different input.

### What's on each competitor card

The shared page displays a competitor's name and any stored website URL, with a **Remove** control. It does not offer the tabbed view's editable website and **How we differ** fields.

In the tabbed view, you can edit the URL and differentiator note on a card. If you write a differentiator, use a fact you can substantiate. For example, describe a service you actually provide rather than claiming to be the best. The small site icon is just a visual aid.

### Saving your changes (batch save)

On the shared page, click **Save changes** and wait for **Business info saved**. This saves the edited page, including both lists. There is no separate audience save there.

The tabbed **Competitors** view uses **Save** and **Cancel** for its local draft. Wait for **Competitors updated** after saving. Cancel restores the saved list. If an update fails, review the list and retry; a visible edit alone does not confirm persistence.

### How competitor data is used

Blog generation accepts competitor details as part of its business context. Keep that information accurate, and review any comparison in the generated draft. A saved note supports an instruction to the writer; it does not verify a claim about another business or guarantee a distinctive article.

Use [Content Plans & Keywords](/content-seo/content-plans) to manage the topics and keywords you intend to target.

## Target audiences (ICP)

An audience segment describes who you serve. ICP means Ideal Customer Profile. A useful segment names a customer group with a recognizable need; an illustrative label is “Homeowners replacing an aging heating system.” Use a group that matches your actual business.

The shared page can display descriptions already stored with audience segments. Its add control creates a segment name, rather than opening a description dialog.

### Adding an audience

Type a name in **Add an audience segment**, then press **Enter** or click **Add**. Click **Save changes** to save the shared page. New segments are created as active.

In the tabbed **Audience** view, **Add** opens a dialog for a required name and an optional description. Confirming the dialog sends the update; there is no later page-level Save step in that view.

### Editing an audience

The shared page offers add and remove controls, rather than a pencil editor. Avoid removing a segment just to experiment with its name: a newly added row does not carry the old row's description.

If you are using the tabbed **Audience** view, its pencil control edits the name and description. Click **Save Changes** in the dialog and wait for **Audiences updated**. Check shared Business Info separately before assuming it reflects that edit.

### Removing or turning off an audience

On the shared page, use **Remove**, then **Save changes**, to remove a segment. That page does not expose an active toggle.

In the tabbed **Audience** view, the X removes a segment; the toggle changes its active state while retaining its name and description. Both actions send updates immediately. Do not treat the presence of a toggle as a guarantee that an existing content plan has changed.

### How audiences shape your content

Audience information supplies context about the people your business serves. Check that generated content addresses their actual needs and uses language they understand. Saving a segment does not guarantee that every draft will target it or rewrite existing posts.

## Who can make changes

The shared page's editing controls and sidebar entry are shown to project editors, admins and owners. If the controls are unavailable, ask the project owner to check your role.

## Before you can save

Shared Business Info can save a profile without a completed website analysis. The tabbed Content SEO audience and competitor forms require a completed analysis; an analysis error there is not a reason to discard edits in the shared page.

Save before running **Re-scan website**, then check the resulting lists. Do not rely on a re-scan to preserve every correction or remember every removal. The two views can retain different information, so verify the shared profile before generating content.
