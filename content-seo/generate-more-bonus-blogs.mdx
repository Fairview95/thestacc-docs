**Generate More** adds bonus blog ideas to the selected project, then attempts to plan their titles and related fields. It does not immediately write and publish a batch of articles. The ideas first appear in **Awaiting date**, where you choose a date for each one.

Use this flow when you need additional topics within the project's available planning capacity. Check the returned result before requesting another batch: ideas can be created successfully even when their title planning fails.

## What Generate More does

The request has separate creation and planning steps. It creates placeholders in **Awaiting date** (`awaiting_user_date`), then attempts to fill their titles, keywords, descriptions, types, and categories using the project's business grounding.

A successful planning step gives you ideas to review before scheduling. A partial or failed planning step can leave placeholders that still need a topic. Neither outcome means that full article text or images have been generated.

Awaiting-date ideas are excluded from the ordinary calendar view and automatic generation. They have a system placeholder date, but no date has yet been committed by you. See [Picking a publish date](#picking-a-publish-date) for the step that makes an idea eligible for the normal workflow.

## Where to find it

On the **Content SEO** dashboard, select the project and look for **Generate more (up to N)**. The number shown comes from that project's planning headroom. Open the dialog, inspect its count, enter the number you want, and submit once.

The button can be hidden while data loads, when headroom is unavailable, for particular plan codes, for an unlimited configuration, when no planning room remains, or when the current window has no remaining days. Its absence does not establish which of those conditions applies.

## What you need before it will work

<h3 id="an-active-plan-that-covers-today">Eligible access and a usable content plan</h3>

The server checks project access, the owning account's Content SEO activation, applicable plan features, and planning capacity. A collaborator's personal plan does not replace the owning project's configuration. The action requires the appropriate editing permission.

The flow also needs a content plan to attach the ideas to. If it reports that no active content plan is available, inspect [Content Plans & Keywords](/content-seo/content-plans) before trying another batch. A plan displayed in your account is not, by itself, proof that this project's request meets every eligibility check.

### Available headroom

Planning headroom is the applicable allowance minus blogs counted as planned in the current quota cycle. The current cycle is resolved from configuration; it is not universally the entire subscription billing window.

Check [Quota & Limits](/content-seo/quota-and-limits) to distinguish planning headroom from generation usage. A request can be rejected even with positive headroom if access, plan availability, or another request limit blocks it.

<h2 id="bonus-blogs-count-against-your-quota">
Planning and generation use different counters
</h2>

Bonus placeholders can occupy planning room before they consume a generation allowance. Generation usage is counted separately from planning headroom, using the blog’s consumption timestamp. Do not interpret an added idea as a completed article or assume that moving its calendar date erases generation already recorded.

Choosing a later date can change which cycle counts that idea as planned. It does not establish paid access for the future date or promise that the worker will generate it then. Check the applicable access, quota, and automation settings when that date arrives.

## Limits on how many you can add

The current backend enforces both planning headroom and a maximum of **200 blogs per request**. The dashboard's displayed maximum is based on headroom and can be higher than that server cap. If a request is rejected for exceeding the cap, use a smaller count; do not assume the displayed number overrides the server.

A request under the numerical limit still needs to pass the other checks. Adding ideas and filling their titles also takes variable time. This guide does not promise that a larger batch completes as quickly as a smaller one.

## Picking a publish date

1. Find the idea in the dashboard's **Pick a date** panel for bonus blogs.
2. Review its title and keyword, including any incomplete planning warning.
3. Choose today or a future date in the date picker.
4. Click **Set date** and inspect the result.

On success, the idea leaves **Awaiting date**, becomes **To be generated**, and joins the regular calendar. That date makes it eligible for generation, subject to the project's settings and checks. It is not confirmation that the article is already written or live.

Near a timezone boundary, the server can normalize a just-past local date to its current date. Check the saved date after committing it. The date picker does not impose a future billing-window maximum, but accepting a future date does not certify future subscription coverage.

If another action changed the idea while you were saving, refresh before trying again. If it remains in Awaiting date, read the error rather than assuming the date was saved.

## If titles did not finish generating

A warning can mean the placeholders were added but their topic planning was incomplete. Inspect the existing Awaiting date list before submitting another Generate More request, so you do not add another batch unnecessarily.

An incomplete title can appear as an awaiting-title or placeholder label. The article body has not been generated at this stage. Check the specific affected idea rather than treating a partial planning result as a complete batch.

### How to recover

When you click **Set date** for an unplanned idea, the current server attempts to complete its topic planning before activating it. That fallback needs eligible generation access and business grounding. If planning fails, the date is not committed and the idea remains awaiting a date.

Read the returned message. If business grounding is missing, update the project's setup or seek help with the analysis before retrying. If planning is temporarily unavailable, inspect the idea after the error and retry the date-setting action when appropriate. Do not assume a missing title will always be repaired later by automatic generation.

If the same ideas repeatedly fail, provide support with the project, affected ideas, and sanitized error. Keep credentials and private customer material out of screenshots.

## Quick reference

- **Generate More:** creates bonus ideas and attempts topic planning.
- **Awaiting date:** keeps ideas out of automatic generation until you commit a date successfully.
- **Set date:** can include topic planning when the idea is incomplete; an error can leave it awaiting.
- **To be generated:** means the idea is ready for a generation attempt, not that an article has been delivered.
