The Promotions Calendar is retired in the current Content SEO interface. Older Promotions links now lead to the Business Details, Audience and Competitors tabs. Use Content Plan to review topics, Business Info for current facts and User Direction for a seasonal angle. Review offers manually before publishing.

<span id="turn-on-promotions-for-a-month" aria-hidden="true" style={{scrollMarginTop: "8rem"}}></span>

## The monthly promotion controls are retired

The old **Run promotions this month** toggle and **Generate Promotion Plan** workflow are no longer a current settings task. Opening an older Promotions link takes you to business information instead. A redirected settings link does not generate events, activate a month or save a promotion plan.

If you need an earlier saved offer or promotion plan, ask support to check the specific project. The current settings route does not provide that historical calendar. Do not assume old settings were deleted, transferred or can be restored.

## Keep business facts and offers current

Use **Business Info** for your business details, audience and locations. These facts provide context for later writing. The older Content SEO business-information page has **Business Details**, **Audience** and **Competitors** tabs; it is the destination for the retired Promotions links.

Confirm an event's date and location with its organizer before using it in an article. Check the offer's landing page and terms with the person responsible for the promotion. Those are editorial checks you must perform; the retired calendar is not an event-verification service.

## Plan a seasonal blog using current controls

Open **Content Plan** and inspect a suitable topic and date. For an editable **To be generated** row, project editors can change the title, keyword, type and date. Review the saved plan before generating the article. See [Content Plans & Keywords](/content-seo/content-plans) for editing and refresh actions.

For one post's angle, open its detail page and use **Your instructions for this post** before content exists, when the control is available. Click **Save** and wait for **Saved**. Typing alone does not save the direction. See [User Direction](/content-seo/user-direction) for eligibility, limits and clearing instructions.

An illustrative instruction could be: *Explain how customers can prepare their equipment before the local festival. Leave prices and discounts out of the article.* This is an example of direction, not a real event, customer result or observed generated post.

Saved direction guides the writer; it does not guarantee the requested angle or correct facts. Review the generated draft against the intended audience and the evidence you supplied. It does not restore the old event cards, relevance scores, reasoning panels or automatically generated promotion ideas.

## Check dates, saved instructions and the finished draft

Use Content Plan's month arrows to inspect planned posts. Confirm the date on the intended row, then check any saved per-post instructions before generation. The former monthly promotion **Save** and **Cancel** workflow is not part of the current Promotions route.

A calendar date does not guarantee an article will be finished or published in time for an event. Check [Scheduling & Missed Posts](/content-seo/scheduling-missed-posts) and the project's publishing setup. If an offer has ended, update or remove its wording before the article goes live.

## Review promotional wording before publishing

The writer is instructed not to invent promotion codes, prices or offers. Its current user prompt also prohibits specific discount percentages, even though another instruction allows certain supplied promotional details. Treat an exact offer as wording you need to review manually; User Direction is not a reliable guarantee that an exact code or percentage will appear.

Use [Sources & Brand Voice](/content-seo/sources-brand-voice) for supporting material and [Preferences](/content-seo/preferences) for CTA link guidance. Supplied facts and links can help guide writing, but neither a saved instruction nor a completed draft certifies the offer's accuracy or approval.

Before approving or publishing, check the event, dates, landing-page destination, discount terms and any required signoff. Read the actual article, including its images and links. See [Blog Generation](/content-seo/blog-generation) for the writing workflow and [Draft & Approval Workflow](/content-seo/draft-approval-workflow) for review.
