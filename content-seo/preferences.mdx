Preferences guide the writing, images and optional content in blogs for the selected project. Open **Preferences**, choose the **Writing**, **Images** or **Content** tab, and check how each control saves. Saved settings influence future generation; they do not guarantee tone, length, image appearance or factual accuracy.

Choose the correct project before editing. Project editors can change these settings; viewers have read-only controls. Changing a preference does not rewrite an article that already exists.

## How changes save

Style tiles and content toggles send an update when you select them. Fields with their own buttons need a separate action. Typing into a form alone does not save its contents.

If you see **Failed to update preferences**, check the displayed values and retry. The view can change while an update is pending, so a changed selection alone is not confirmation that it saved. Reopen the page after a failed or interrupted update to check the saved state.

## Writing tab: writing style and tone

Choose a general writing style in **Writing Style & Tone**. The selected tile shows a checkmark. **Professional** is shown when no style has been saved.

The available labels are **Informative**, **Simple & Clear**, **Formal**, **Casual**, **Enthusiastic**, **Persuasive**, **Professional**, **Friendly**, **Entertaining**, **Inspirational**, **Analytical** and **Narrative**. Use the tile descriptions to choose the tone closest to your intended audience.

For guidance drawn from your own writing, use **Business Info > Sources > Brand voice**. The writer is instructed to favor an analyzed voice profile over the generic writing-style label when they conflict. Review the generated draft to check the tone and terminology. See [Sources & Brand Voice](/content-seo/sources-brand-voice) for the sample and analysis workflow.

The Writing tab also has **Article Length**. See [the length targets below](#a-note-on-article-length) before choosing a target solely to make a draft longer.

## Images tab: image style

Select an **Image Style** to guide the prompts for generated blog images. **Photorealistic** is shown when no image style has been saved. The other options are **Flat Illustration**, **Cinematic**, **Watercolor**, **Sketch** and **Brand & Text**.

The thumbnails illustrate the style choices. They are not live previews of the images that will appear in your article. Check the generated image for the intended look, readable text and accurate business details before publishing. Selecting Brand & Text alone does not confirm that your logo or an exact brand font will appear.

See [Image Generation](/content-seo/image-generation) for reviewing images, and **Sources > Brand images** for the separate image and logo controls.

## Content tab: what goes inside each post

Use the Content tab for inclusion preferences, link inputs, topics to avoid and a locked footer.

### Content options (toggles)

The inclusion options are **Include YouTube Videos**, **Include CTAs**, **Include Internal Links** and **Include Competitor Links**. With no saved setting, the first three default to on and competitor links default to off.

The YouTube, CTA and internal-link toggles also show or hide their configuration sections. Turning one off hides its form without deleting the saved inputs. It does not remove content from a post that has already been generated.

### YouTube channel

With **Include YouTube Videos** on, use **Add Channel URL** or **Edit** to enter your channel address, then click **Save**. The form trims surrounding whitespace and accepts an empty value to clear it. A nonempty address must start with `http://` or `https://`.

Check the saved address after editing. Saving a channel address does not guarantee that a relevant video from that channel will be included in a generated post. Review any embedded video for relevance and the intended source.

### CTA Links

With **Include CTAs** on, click **Add CTA Link**. Enter a **Label** and **URL**, choose **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Social** or **YouTube** as the type, and add optional **Notes**. Use **Add Link** for a new entry or **Save Changes** when editing one. **Cancel** discards the unsaved form.

The current add/edit form accepts a URL beginning with `http://`, `https://`, `mailto:` or `tel:`. It trims the label, address and notes. Older saved links can use forms accepted elsewhere, including a relative path or anchor; the current form may reject those when you edit them. Use a supported address and check the saved link.

CTA types and notes give placement guidance. Check the finished article for the links and placement you intended. If saving fails because a destination is missing, fix the address and confirm the saved list before generating.

### Your Website Pages

With **Include Internal Links** on, add a page title and URL under **Your Website Pages**, then click **Add Page**. The form accepts addresses starting with `http://`, `https://` or `/`. If you leave the title blank, it uses the address as the title.

Use pages that are useful to the intended reader, and check that the generated links reach the right destination. See [Internal Linking](/content-seo/internal-linking) for how the available pages and blog links are used.

### Locked blog footer

Use **Locked blog footer** for fixed text or simple HTML, such as a standard link. Click **Save footer** and wait for **Blog footer saved**. **Reset** restores the saved value in the form; it does not save a blank footer.

The editor has a **10,000-character** limit. The backend trims and cleans the saved HTML, so review the saved value and the rendered footer. Keep markup simple. A footer is separate from an AI-written CTA and does not establish that a disclaimer meets your professional requirements.

## Topics to avoid

Enter a topic and press **Enter** or click **Add**. Remove a saved topic with the × on its chip. The form ignores a case-insensitive duplicate and waits for an in-progress update before accepting another change.

You can add up to **50 topics**. Saving trims each entry and retains at most **120 characters** per topic. Keep a topic concise and check the saved chip after adding long text. At the list limit, the form keeps your unadded input and shows an error.

The writer receives these topics as guidance to avoid discussing them. This is not a guaranteed filter. Review the draft for excluded subjects, especially when an omission matters for a regulated claim or a sensitive business detail. See [Content Safety & Integrity Guardrails](/content-seo/content-safety-guardrails) for the separate safeguards and their limits.

## A note on article length

**Article Length** in the Writing tab offers the following targets. **Standard** is shown when no length has been saved. Selecting a tile saves the preference, which the generation flow passes to the writer as a target range.

These are generation instructions, not an enforced final count or evidence of better rankings. Choose the target that fits the reader's task, then check the finished article for missing explanations or repetition. See [Word Count Targets](/content-seo/word-count-targets) for reviewing draft length.

Preferences also does not replace the keyword selected for a post. Use [Content Plans & Keywords](/content-seo/content-plans) for topics and keywords, and [User Direction](/content-seo/user-direction) for instructions specific to one post.

## Advanced and stored brand settings

Review your business's brand information in [Business Info](/content-seo/business-setup). Preferences shows the Writing and Images choices described above. A stored thumbnail, infographic or font value does not establish that a separate control is available here.
