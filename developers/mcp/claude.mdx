Works on Claude's web app, desktop app and mobile app (connectors you add on the web show up on every device). You need a theStacc account and to be the owner or an admin of the project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    In Claude, go to **Customize > Connectors**, click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add theStacc">
    Name it **theStacc**, paste this address and click **Add**:

    ```
    https://mcp.thestacc.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with theStacc">
    Click **Connect** next to theStacc. A theStacc page opens in your browser. Log in with your usual account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a project and permissions">
    Choose the **one project** Claude should work on. Reading and drafting are ticked. Tick **Publish approved posts** only if you want Claude to publish what you approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Click **Approve**. You are sent back to Claude, connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, an organization owner adds the custom connector first (**Organization settings > Connectors**). Members then click **Connect** next to it under **Customize > Connectors**.

## Use it in a chat

Click **+** at the bottom left of the message box, open **Connectors** and switch theStacc on. Then try:

- *"What can you do with theStacc?"*
- *"Review my content plan and write the next blog."*
- *"Create a LinkedIn post about our new opening hours."*
- *"Draft a Google Business post about our weekend offer."*

Claude asks before running tools that change things. Read-only tools (lists, reports) are marked as such.

## Disconnect

In theStacc, open **Content SEO > Settings > Publishing > Agent access**, find **MCP: Claude** and click **Revoke**. Claude stops working with your project straight away.
