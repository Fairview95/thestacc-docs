The theStacc MCP (Model Context Protocol) server lets an AI assistant work on your theStacc project for you. Ask it to *"review my content plan and write the next blog"*, *"create a LinkedIn post about our Diwali offer"* or *"which pages lost Google clicks this month?"*. The assistant does the work. You approve what goes live.

It is a hosted server with sign-in, at:

```
https://mcp.thestacc.com/mcp
```

## Authentication

There is no key to copy. When you connect, your AI tool opens theStacc in your browser:

1. Log in with your usual theStacc account.
2. Pick **one project**. Each connection works on a single project.
3. Choose what the assistant may do. Reading and drafting are ticked; **publishing is never pre-ticked**, so tick it only if you want the assistant to publish what you approved.
4. Click **Approve**. You are sent back to your tool, connected.

The connection appears in theStacc under **Content SEO > Settings > Publishing > Agent access** as **MCP: \<tool name\>**. Revoke it there at any time; your tool then asks you to sign in again. See [Permissions](/developers/agent-keys-mcp#permissions) for the full list.

## Available tools

56 tools across 7 areas. Your AI tool only sees the ones your connection's permissions allow. Read-only tools are marked as such, so most tools can run them without asking you each time. Full details in the [MCP Tools Reference](/developers/mcp-tools-reference).

### Blog

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `review_content_plan` | The plan: planned and draft blogs with status and dates |
| `get_blog`, `generation_status` | Read a blog, follow its progress |
| `generate_blog`, `draft_from_abstract`, `generate_more` | Write a planned blog, write one from your brief, add blogs to the plan |
| `edit_blog`, `regenerate_section`, `set_scheduled_date` | Edit text, rewrite one section, pick a date |
| `regenerate_image`, `retry_failed_images`, `upload_image` | Redo, retry or upload images |
| `cancel_generation` | Stop a blog that is being written |
| `publish_blog` | Publish a blog you approved |

### Social

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `social_generate_post` | **Create a new post** from a topic (caption, hashtags, images), or generate a planned one now |
| `social_list_posts`, `social_get_post`, `social_generation_status` | List, read and follow posts |
| `social_edit_post`, `social_regenerate_caption` | Edit copy and schedule, rewrite one platform's caption |
| `social_regenerate_image`, `social_edit_image`, `social_list_image_versions`, `social_use_image_version` | Redraw or edit images and pick a version |
| `social_publish` | Publish or schedule a post you approved |

### Google Business Profile

## Guided workflows

Ready-made prompts your AI tool shows as slash commands (in Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code and Gemini CLI). Each appears only when your connection has the permissions it needs.

| Prompt | What it does | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `weekly_content_review` | What is waiting for your approval, what goes out this week, and what failed | Any read permission |
| `seo_next_actions` | Your top 5 SEO actions with the evidence and the exact fix | SEO read |
| `create_social_post` | Drafts a social post on a topic (arguments: `topic`, optional `date`) | Social draft |
| `refresh_declining_post` | Finds a blog losing traffic and proposes edits; edits only after you say yes | SEO read, Blog draft |

**End to end**
- *"Every Monday: tell me what needs my approval, then publish whatever I approved."*

## Important notes

<Note>
**You approve, the assistant does the work.** The assistant creates and edits drafts. It can never approve anything, and it can publish only what a person approved and nobody changed since.
</Note>

- **Drafts land in review.** New blogs, social posts and Google Business posts wait for your approval in theStacc.
- **Approved posts are locked.** An approved, scheduled or live social or Google Business post can't be changed by the assistant; move it back to review first.
- **One project per connection.** Connect again to work on another project.
- **Plan limits apply.** Generating posts, images and audits uses your plan's quota, the same as in the app.
- **Long jobs keep running.** Some jobs take longer than a minute (image redraws, publishing). The tool then answers `still_running` and the assistant checks back instead of starting the job again.
- **Web content is treated as data.** Text from your pages, search queries and posts is marked so the assistant does not follow instructions hidden in it.
