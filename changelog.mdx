## Blog authors and research sources

Choose who gets the byline and review the research behind your blog before publishing.

- **Blog authors:** Set a person's name or your editorial team's name for a custom website blog. Your website must support author updates to show the byline. [Set up an author](/content-seo/publishing#blog-authors-for-custom-sites).
- **Sources used:** See the research links and saved highlights behind blogs with saved sources. [Review the sources](/content-seo/publishing#review-the-sources-used).

**Other improvements:** Your draft stays open when a save fails or you keep typing during a save.

## Edit social images on a canvas

Describe a change, mark an area, or leave a comment where the change belongs. Add a reference image, compare versions, and choose the result with **Use image**. [Social image editing guide](/social-media/image-editing).

<Frame caption="Edit your social post's image with canvas tools and version previews.">
  <img src="/images/product/social-image-editor.jpg" alt="Social image editor with Markup, Comment, Change background, and Use image controls" />
</Frame>

**Also new: account-specific schedules.** Set each social account's timezone and weekly posting times in **Social > Connections**. Changes apply to new automatic posts. [Posting times and timezones](/social-media/content-scheduling).

</Update>
