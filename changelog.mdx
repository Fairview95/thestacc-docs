## Awaiting release: blog authors and research sources

These changes are prepared for review. They will become available after the backend and Content Hub updates are deployed.

### Choose a blog author

Set a person's name or an organization such as your Editorial Team from the blog's author settings in Content Hub. Reset the setting to use the default author again. WordPress blogs continue to use the connected site's existing author selection.

Custom website integrations receive an optional `author` field when a blog is published or updated. Existing blogs without a custom author retain their default behavior.

**Website action:** Support both `blog.published` and `blog.updated`, save the author before acknowledging the event, and keep the visible byline and structured data consistent. See [custom webhook setup](/integrations/custom-webhook) and the [author field reference](/developers/webhooks/field-reference).

### Review the sources used for a blog

Open **Sources used** in the Content Hub blog review or editor to inspect the research links, highlights, and available dates. The popup is shown when the blog has saved research sources; older blogs without those records do not show invented sources.

**Your action:** Review the supporting sources before approving a blog. See [reviewing and publishing blogs](/content-seo/publishing).

### Safer editing and saves

The editor stays open when a final save fails or you continue typing while the save is in progress. Concurrent author and content updates detect conflicts instead of silently overwriting a newer save.

**Your action:** If a save reports a conflict, review the latest blog and retry your change.
