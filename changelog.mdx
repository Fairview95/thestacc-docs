The latest updates to help you create, review, and publish your content.

## October 6, 2026

### Blog authors and research sources

- **Choose a blog author:** Use a person's name or your editorial team's name for blogs sent to a custom website. Open a blog in Content Hub, enter the name, choose the author type, and save. Your website must support author updates to show the byline. [Author setup](/content-seo/publishing#blog-authors-for-custom-sites).
- **Review Sources used:** Open the research links and saved highlights behind a blog before approving it. Available for blogs with saved research sources. [Review sources](/content-seo/publishing#review-the-sources-used).
- **Safer saves:** The editor keeps your draft open when saving fails or you keep typing during a save.

## October 5, 2026

### Edit social images on a canvas

Open a social post and click **Edit image**. Describe a change, mark an area, or place a comment exactly where the change belongs. Add a reference image when you want to guide the result.

Compare the generated versions, then click **Use image** to choose the one for your post. [Social image editing guide](/social-media/image-editing).
