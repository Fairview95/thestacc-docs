theStacc Assistant is a chat helper that sits on every page of the app. It keeps an eye on your blog, social and Google Business posts, tells you what needs you, and makes changes when you ask. Nothing is changed, approved or published until you say so.

## Where to find it

1. Look for the round **Ask theStacc** button in the bottom-right corner of any page.
2. A red badge on the button means something needs you, for example a post that failed or posts waiting for review.
3. Click the button. The panel opens on the right side of the page. The page stays usable next to it.

<Frame caption="The red badge and status pill show when posts need you.">
  <img src="/images/product/assistant-launcher-badge.jpg" alt="Ask theStacc button with a 9+ badge and a pill reading 1 failed, 31 to review" />
</Frame>

The panel header shows which page it is looking at, for example **On Content Hub** or **On Blog Editor**. Whatever you have open on that page is what the assistant works on by default.

- **New chat** starts a fresh conversation.
- The clock icon opens your earlier chats.
- **Speak** lets you talk instead of type.

## You stay in control

- It never approves or publishes anything without your OK. Changes show up as a card first, and you tap **Approve** to save them.
- It works on the project you have selected.
- If it is not sure what you mean, it asks one simple question instead of guessing.
