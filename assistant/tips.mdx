---
title: "Tips for Asking"
description: "How to ask the assistant for what you want, and what it will and will not do on its own."
---

You do not need special words. Ask the way you would ask a person on your team.

## Keep it short

Short requests work well:

- "make this shorter"
- "seo?"
- "pic is too dark"
- "fix the title"
- "move it to next friday"

Typos are fine, and so is Hinglish, for example "isko thoda chhota karo".

## The open post is the default

If you have a post open, the assistant works on that post unless you name a different one. On a list page, such as Content Hub, name the post or tap a post chip in the chat first.

## You can ask for more than one thing

"Change the title to something catchier, add an FAQ at the end and schedule it for Monday" works. The assistant shows you the changes before anything is saved.

## When it is not sure, it asks

If your request could mean more than one thing, for example "the other one" or "fix it", the assistant asks one simple question, often with buttons to tap. It does not change anything until it knows what you want.

## Approving changes

- Changes come as an approval card. Tap **Approve** to save, or **Cancel** to leave things as they are.
- Typing "yes" or "ok" in the chat does not approve a card. Tap the **Approve** button. This keeps you from approving something by accident.
- For images, choose **Use this**, **Keep the old one** or **Try again**.

## What it never does on its own

- It never publishes or approves a post without your OK.
- It never posts to your website or social accounts behind your back.
- It does not make up facts about your business. If it needs something only you know, it asks.

## Things to know

- Some answers take a few seconds. Making a new image takes about a minute.
- If something goes wrong, the chat shows **Retry** and **Dismiss**. Try again, or ask in a different way.
- Use **New chat** to start fresh, and the clock icon to go back to an earlier chat.
- If the assistant cannot do something, it tells you, and points you to where you can do it in the app.

## Good things to try first

- "What needs my attention?"
- "What's left to set up?"
- "Ask me my writer's questions"
- "Shorten my next social caption"
- "Why did this one fail?"
